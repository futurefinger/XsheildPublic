LEGAL & PRIVACY DISCLOSURE

XShield Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 • Published by Future Finger Technologies, New Delhi, India

1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy

At Future Finger Technologies, we believe cybersecurity should safeguard your system without invading your personal life. Traditional security tools often harvest browsing history, transmit confidential files to third-party cloud servers, and monetize user telemetry.

XShield Antivirus was architected under a strict Privacy-First paradigm: all file scanning, threat analysis, quarantine operations, and heuristic validations happen 100% locally on your device. We do not inspect personal documents, collect browsing activities, or maintain tracking databases.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Files or Documents: Your photos, documents, spreadsheets, and private folders are never transmitted over the internet.
  • No Web Browsing Telemetry: XShield does not monitor your browser history, bookmarks, search queries, or web interactions.
  • No Keystroke or Screen Logging: We do not log keystrokes, clipboard contents, or display frames.
  • No Device Profiling or Advertising IDs: We do not generate advertiser identifiers or sell demographic profiles.

3. What Data Is Processed Locally

To provide real-time protection and antivirus scanning, XShield processes file metadata (such as SHA-256 hashes, file sizes, and PE header signatures) strictly in local machine memory. Quarantined threats are encrypted and saved solely in your local storage directory (C:\ProgramData\XShield\quarantine) with zero external transmission.

4. Software Updates & Definition Checks

When you manually or automatically check for updates, XShield connects to our official GitHub repository (futurefinger/XsheildPublic) to check the latest version tag. This request contains standard HTTP headers (such as IP address for packet delivery) and transmits no personal data or file telemetry.

5. Compliance with Indian IT Act 2000

This privacy disclosure complies with Section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.
